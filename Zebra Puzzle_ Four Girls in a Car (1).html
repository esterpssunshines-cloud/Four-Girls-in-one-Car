🚗 Four Girls in a Car

Zebra puzzle · Easy level · Prepositions of place

The story

Four girls are going on a road trip. Each girl has one thing in her hand and one thing in her bag. Find out who is who!

Names: Mia, Zoe, Lily, Ruby
In her hand: a phone, a sandwich, a water bottle, a map
In her bag: a book, an umbrella, a camera, a hat

“In front of” and “behind” mean directly in front or directly behind (same side of the car).

The clues

  1. Mia is sitting in the front, on the left.
  2. Zoe is sitting next to Mia.
  3. Lily is sitting behind Mia.
  4. The girl with the phone in her hand is sitting in front of the girl with the water bottle in her hand.
  5. The sandwich is in the hand of the girl sitting next to Mia.
  6. The camera is in the bag of the girl sitting behind Zoe.
  7. The girl with the umbrella in her bag is sitting next to the girl with the phone in her hand.
  8. The book is in the bag of the girl sitting in front of Lily.
